How it works

  1. Upload a .3dm file. It is analysed for common problems.
  2. Clean up: move it to the origin, remove stray / invalid / duplicate objects and slim overly dense meshes. Download a fixed .3dm.
  3. 3D print: choose a printer and final size and get a watertight .3mf / .stl ready for Bambu Studio.

Files are processed locally on this computer.
